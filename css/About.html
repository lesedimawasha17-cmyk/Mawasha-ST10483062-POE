Our Story

Jacky's Restaurant is a restaurant in Aqua Park, Tzaneen, Limpopo, South Africa. The restaurant is located at 5 Douglas Street.

The restaurant opened in late December 2025 and runs under new management in a space that was previously occupied by another restaurant.

It grew into a local aesthetic hotspot for breakfast, grills, and social events.

Our Mission

This restaurant's mission is to give customers affordable meals, a pleasant dining experience, and excellent customer service in a welcoming enviroment.

Our Vision

This restaurant's vision is to rank among the community's most reputable and well-liked eateries,to become a well-loved community resturant known for affordable food and friendly service

Our Target Audience

The target audience includes locals, visitors, working professionals, families, and students.
